# PRODUCT.md — STARHOPPER Flight Deck

register: brand

## Product purpose

An immersive, mobile-first **starship cockpit** web experience: a shared imaginative
toy for a **dad and his two young boys to play during car rides**. The phone is mounted
on the dash; tapping instruments, switching flight modes, and "tuning signals" turns a
boring drive into piloting a family exploration starship. It is a *feeling*, not a tool.

This is a personal side project. It is NOT Florian P. Consulting branding. None of the
FP brand rules (purple, Bricolage/Poppins, Brand Therapy voice) apply here.

## Users

- A father and his ~5–10 year old boys, together, in a moving car.
- Used one-handed / thumb-first, often glanced at in low light (dusk, night drives).
- Short attention, high delight expectation. They want sound, motion, and the fantasy
  of being a crew. They are NOT operating a real instrument; immersion beats utility.

## Voice and tone

Adventurous, warm, cinematic, trustworthy. A friendly ship AI and a confident pilot's
deck. **Never scary, aggressive, violent, or loud.** Combat is "boss-fight exciting,"
not war. Wonder over threat.

## Anti-references (if it looks like any of these, it has failed)

- A soundboard or a grid of buttons.
- A 1990s Flash website.
- Arcade neon-on-black; glowing-blob HUD; the Orbitron/Eurostile sci-fi cliché.
- Generic HUD motion: concentric pulsing rings, orbiting circles, glowing blobs as the
  main motif, stock radar sweeps with no material.
- A "toy demo" with fake beep/oscillator sound. All audio is real recorded/voiced files.

## Strategic principles

1. **Two hero interactions, done excellently** (mode transition + signal-triangulation
   pad) beat twenty weak controls.
2. **Material instrumentation:** depth, bezels, machined surfaces, believable readouts.
   The screen is a built object, not flat graphics.
3. **Mobile-first, one-thumb, car-safe.** Big targets, no precision required, no reading.
4. **Real audio only.** Cinematic music beds, real SFX, a consistent robot voice.
5. **Family-safe by construction.** Exciting, kind, never frightening.
6. **Ship-able statically** to GitHub Pages. Light enough for a phone.
